Haulotte PULSAR DTC — Weekly Cockpit Operating Guide

1. Weekly Review Workflow

Every week, start at the Dashboard to answer four primary questions:

  • Where are we today? Check overall progress across the 6 lanes and reporting coverage.
  • What changed since previous review? Click "Save Weekly Snapshot" to see automated deltas (progress gained, dates shifted, prototypes received).
  • What needs attention? Review late or blocked items and overdue prototype deliveries.
  • What controls delivery? Inspect zero-float activities on the critical paths to Mainstream and Steering Serial TR.

2. Updating Purchasing & Automatic Roadmap Sync

When prototype dates change in Purchasing, the linked Roadmap prototype milestones update automatically so you never enter dates twice. A partial receipt does not mark a gate complete: verify physical receipt and check "Accepted for assembly/test" to clear the gate.

3. "Must Follow" Dependency Rules

Only connect genuine physical handoffs (design approval before procurement, prototype delivery before assembly/testing, validation closure before technical release). Circular dependencies are rejected automatically by cycle detection algorithms.

4. Critical Path & Float Interpretation

Activities with Total Float ≤ 0 are on the critical path. The system explains each with clear notifications such as "A delay here moves Steering delivery." Missing information produces a Critical path incomplete diagnostic rather than erroneous scheduling.

5. Independent Mainstream & Steering Endpoints

Mainstream Serial TR and Steering Serial TR operate independently. Narrow and Wide models share the same planning scope in the Steering lane with dedicated variant checklists, preserving the physical sequence: chassis → pivots → cylinder & steering mechanism → hoses.
